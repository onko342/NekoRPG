  • Currency:
    • 1000 C = 1 X, 1000 X = 1 Z, 1000 Z = 1 D, 1000 D = 1 B.

    • All of the merchants here are evil businessmen, sell prices are far greater than buy prices (normally over 200%)
      The "Haggling" skill can decrease sell prices to a degree (2% per level, compounding)
  • Market Saturation
    • When you sell large amounts of monster materials, the price will slowly decrease, until it reaches 10% of the original price. This affects all merchants.
      Prices recover over time, but this is a very slow process.

  • Crafting Categories:
    • Items: Most are boring stuff, such as consumables or ingredients that are further crafted
    • Components: They can be taken a step further and assembled into equipment
      Their quality isn't directly moved to equipment stats, their stats will be shown as a "base value"
      Which is their stats at 100% quality.
    • Equipment: What you can actually wear, most are composed of components.
      Quality is affected by skill level and component level.
  • You can acquire items through passive events and combat, items that can be used in crafting often mention their uses in their description.
    For passive events, time needed, success rate, and the amount of items acquired aare all affected by skill levels.
  • Quality and Quality Cap
    • Equipment quality will fall within a certain range; they will also be corrected by a quality cap. Usually, equipment quality is negatively correlated with component level, positively correlated with workbench level, and positively correlated with skill level.   • AGI / Agility:
    • The sum of all skills/environments that affect accuracy/dodging.
    • Calculations are nonlinear.
    • AGI=5*enemy AGI, accuracy 98.0%
    • AGI=2*enemy AGI, accuracy 92.1%
    • AGI=1*enemy AGI, accuracy 78.4%
    • AGI=0.5*enemy AGI, accuracy 50.0%
    • AGI=0.2*enemy AGI, accuracy 15.7%

  • SPD / Attack Speed:
    • Directly converted to character/monster attack speed. 2 * SPD = two attacks are made within the same amount of time.
    • To avoid insanity and potential problems, when new fight begins, both character and enemies have their attack cooldowns scaled up so that no one attacks faster than once per second.

  • Special Skills:
    • Within reasonable ranges, this game will replicate the mechanics of Neko Lulu.
    • For map damage/final damage etc.,
    • the priority is achieving an "equivalent effect"; if this is unachievable, a new effect will be added.

  • Realm:
    • NekoRPG's realms are categorized into the following ranks: Dust, Myriad, Tidal, Earth, etc.
      Dust Rank, the most disadvantaged group, they have a weak constitution.
      Myriad Rank, strong as a cow, bold as a tiger.
      Tidal Rank, detects the elements, divine strength of a thousand tons...
      Above this —— Earth Rank is the commonly accepted first major milestone of a cultivator.
      Only after surpassing this barrier may you walk the continent, and not worry about food.
      Sky Rank counts as the level of an advanced adventurer in Yangang.
      Cloud Rank is the rank of true power in Yangang.
      Domain Rank powerhouses are rare in the entire region.
      World Rank only appears at the faraway [World City].

    • [Mental Weapons] of the same type have the same usage methods.

  • Based on the original work's description of the Xuelo Continent, the Xuelo time system has been built.
    Xuelo Continent's time system is split into: Era-Year-Day-Hour-Minute
    1 hour is 60 minutes, 1 day is 180 hours, 50 days is a year, 10081 years is an era.
    As such, one Xuelo minute is approximately 58.9 Earth seconds.

  • During nighttime in regular areas (150-30 o'clock), and all day in dark areas, Neko will receive an additional agility penalty.
    This penalty will be weakened by "Night Vision" — just staying in such areas will earn you "Night Vision" XP passively.

  • Some combat areas have special qualities, bringing penalties to specific stats.
    Example: Some areas may not have enough room to move, making dodging more difficult.
    They usually have a matching skill to reduce the penalty. Similarly, staying in such an area will passively earn you XP.

  • Explanation:
    • A brand new mechanic added to the Barrier Lake.
    • The bar filling process is the same as woodcutting and mining, but after the bar fills up, the game will enter the tugging screen.
    • Hold the screen to raise the "fishing bar", release to allow it to fall.
    • When the fish is within the "fishing bar" progress increases, the reverse is also true.
    • When progress is full, the fish is caught and provides XP. When progress reaches 0, the fish will get away.

  • Varieties:
    • Lake Carp, no level requirement, the weakest fish.
    • Blue Flower Fish, Lv. 3 required, medium strength fish.
    • Ice Pillar Fish, Lv. 10 required, the strongest fish!
  • 1st Layer - Gem Devourer:
    • Used to clean up useless gems deep in the softcap zone, and provides some bonuses at the same time.
    • Unlocked at Earth Rank Stage 1, devour gems to acquire VP (Value Points).
    • The VP acquisition formula is the gem's G_value ^ 2 / 1e4. The Advanced Red Gem is 1, Advanced Green Gem is 4, Superior Yellow Gem is 25, etc.
    • The VP bonus formula is All Skill XP Multiplier * VP ^ 0.07.
    • Additionally, I took inspiration from Grass Cutting Incremental - Synthesis for the color changes... who can even tell?!

  • 2nd Layer - God of Greed:
    • It would be too difficult if you could only increase Luck through leveling up! It's time to add a new method!
    • Unlocked at Sky Realm Stage 1 , devour currency Treasure Coins or above to increase Luck.
    • Its bonus formula is Luck * Treasure Coins Devoured ^ 0.1.
    • It's still a work in progress, things may be changed in the future. Including the ability to devour Universe Coins.

  • Basic Background:
    • The [Core Reactor] is a piece of [technology] discovered in the Outer-Space Spaceship by Neko.
    • It has good energy reflection abilities, and can coax out the energy in [A7·Energy Cores] and [B1·Energy Cores].
    • This energy can be used to make super high-strength [Gel Sword Hilts], or condensed into [Basic Evolution Crystals].
    • However, make sure that you add some [High-Energy Gel] as a coolant. Or else, overheating the reactor will cause a meltdown.
    • Inspiration Source: HBM core technology - RBMK reactor pile.

  • B1·Energy Core - Breeder Fuel:
    • Increases [Radiation] by the formula 0.4log(n+1)x.
    • Enters a supercritical state when there are [317] in the reactor, causing [Radiation] to grow exponentially.
    • Heat value is [8000], meaning that 1 B1·Energy Core can release 8000 [Energy] when fully depleted.

  • A7·Energy Core - Regular Fuel:
    • Increases reactor [Radiation] by the formula 0.4sqrt(nx).
    • Will never enter a [Supercritical] state. Disregarding other sources of [Radiation], 2% will be used per second.
    • Has a heat value of [20]. Means that 1 A7·Energy Core can release 20 [Energy] when fully depleted.

  • Lightning Blessing - Radiation Source:
    • Releases [Radiation] by the formula 0.001n.
    • Compared to the previous two types of fuel, its greatest advantage is that it doesn't need any [Radiation] to activate.
    • Heat value [1]. When a Lightning Blessing is fully depleted, it will only have released 1 [Energy].
    • Can be used to [fully deplete] the previous two types of fuel, but it will never be [fully depleted] itself.

  • High-Energy Gel - Coolant:
    • Increases the heat capacity of the reactor pile by the formula 0.01n (Ratio between [Radiation] and [Change in temperature])
    • Can also cool the reactor when added in the middle of the reaction. However, some of the energy will be diluted and wasted.
    • The energy dilution mechanic has been removed. Now you can add High-Energy Gel without worry./li>
    • Increasing heat capacity is not without cost. By the square-cube rule, the rate of natural cooling is n^-1/3, decreasing when adding more gel.
    • This means that the reactor cools by -10%/s when there are 10 units of [High-Energy Gel], a figure that diminishes to -1%/s when where are 10000.

  • Final Product - Gel Sword Hilt:
    • Every withdrawal uses 20% of the reactor's [High-Energy Gel] and all of its [Energy].
    • Therefore, just add enough to use, adding too much is a waste.
    • Quality formula is 100+15ln(x+1).
    • Getting an epic, legendary, mythical, transcendental [Gel Sword Hilt]
    • respectively require 55/789/11304/617226 [Energy].
    • Something to note: taking a Gel Sword Hilt requires at least 10 [High Energy Gel] in the reactor.
  • Basic Evolution Crystal:
    • Clearing [Spaceship Core - 3] unlocks [Spaceship Core - Lower Room].
    • Defeating the [Silver Blood-Eye B1 [BOSS]] in [Spaceship Core - Lower Room] yields a large amount of reactor pile materials.
    • At the same time, you can receive [Crafting Basic Evolution Crystals - Learn Instantly]. After this, you will have the capability to make [Basic Evolution Crystals].
    • Creating a [Basic Evolution Crystal] requires 1,000,000 [Primal Energy].
    • Simultaneously, 99% of the reactor pile's High-Energy Gel will be consumed.
    • After the patch, this will only make the reactor pile instantly stop (zero the internal radiation amount)
    • Work toward mass production!

  • Reactor Meltdown:
    • When temperature exceeds 9999°C, the temperature bar will glow blue,
    • and the reactor will meltdown.
    • Although it won't become unusable after blowing up,
    • all items within will be cleared, and you will receive a [Radiation] debuff.
    • Effects: HP regen - 8%, HP Max - 50%.
    • Go clear it while the Moonlight Idol gives you a discount out of pity...
    • Unless you didn't even add gel, in this scenario, the buff is only 100 seconds.

  • Increasing Methods:
    • When using [Basic Evolution Crystals], [XP wasted by realm suppression] increase [Hel Swamp Radiation Spread] at a 10 billion:1% ratio.
    • Basically, Sky Rank Stage 1 ~0/crystal, Sky Rank Stage 2 ~8%/crystal, Sky Rank Stage 3 ~9.6%/crystal.
    • When killing a [Wild Beast], its energies returning to the environment will bring a [0.004%] increase to [Hel Swamp Radiation Spread].
    • It doesn't look like much, but if you oneshotted 3 enemies at a time with 2.0 attack speed, [Hel Swamp Radiation Spread] would increase by 10.8% per Xuelo day.
    • The more the radiation, the stronger the monsters. The stronger the monsters, the slower your grinding your be. The slower you grind, the less the radiation.
    • So, the more the radiation, the less the radiation.
    • Don't think this is funny! This is per☆fect negative feedback design!

  • Decreasing Methods:
    • The only way to decrease it is the passage of time.
    • Every Xuelo day, [Swamp Radiation Spread] decreases by [1% + 1% of current] naturally.
    • To prevent it from appearing in non 3-1 areas, [Swamp Radiation Spread] won't show decrease notifications when less than 1%.
    • If you accidentally ate too many crystals... you can fix it with a nice sleep.
    • The half-life when not sleeping is roughly 2.8 hours, sleeping shortens it to 28 minutes. (both calculations ignore the 1% base, which actually can't be ignored, but that makes the differential equation hard to solve.)

    • Many countries in Asia use a number system distinct from the one western countries are familiar with.
    • Originated in China, it works in increments of 4 digits instead of 3.
    • Much like in word numbers in English, each increment has its own character used to represent it.
